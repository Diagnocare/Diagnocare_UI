Diagnocare · UI redesign proposal

A lab app a first-week receptionist can use

Wireframes for the new Diagnocare layout, based on the ServiceHub reference: a fixed sidebar instead of hover menus, a home page that starts with tasks, and screens that walk through one job at a time. Screen 1 and the sidebar are already built on the ui-redesign-sidebar branch; the rest are the target for the next steps.

Everything is visibleThe sidebar lists every screen the person may open. No menu disappears when the mouse moves.
Start from the taskHome asks "What do you want to do?" with big task buttons and a count of what is waiting.
Words, not iconsEvery button says what it does. Status is colour + dot + word, never colour alone.
One step at a timeLong forms become short steps with a check page before saving.

Why it looks like this

1Sidebar replaces five hover dropdowns. Grouped by job, active page highlighted, counts beside items that have work waiting. Folds into a "Menu" drawer on phones.
2The two things front desk does most are always in the top bar: find a patient and register one.
3Tasks named in the user's words, each with its own colour and a waiting count. Only tasks the role can do are shown.
4The sample pipeline, like ServiceHub's job pipeline. Each number opens the work queue on that step. Problems get their own red line.

Redesign: sidebar, always open

Daily work — Home, Work queue, Patients, Test reports, Bills & receipts, Test reminders
Reports — Business reports (folds open on click)
Lab setup — Tests & prices, Doctors & contacts, Lab profile, Lab settings
Team — Staff, Attendance, Salary, Discount approvals, Holidays, Visit schedule
My things — My attendance, My visits, My salary
  • Every allowed screen visible at once; the current one is highlighted.
  • Each role sees only what its permissions allow (same rules as today).
  • Plain names: "Tests & prices", "Doctors & contacts", "Staff".
  • Works by tap: a Menu button opens it as a drawer on phones and tablets.
